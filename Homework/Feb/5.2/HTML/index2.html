Tomato pasta bake

Make this simple student pasta and tomato dish for a delicious, filling and budget-friendly supper. It is a great way to use up simple storecupboard ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 400g can cherry or chopped tomatoes
  • pinch of sugar

Preparation

  1. Heat the oil in a large saucepan and cook the onion for 5 mins until softened.
  2. Add the garlic and cook for a further minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes, sugar, and some seasoning. Simmer for 15 mins.
